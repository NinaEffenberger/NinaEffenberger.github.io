We have published two new pre-prints on very extreme events! In the
<a href="https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.15284">first one</a>, we introduce a framework to downscale ensemble boosting generated data with machine learning. This can be used to get higher resolution versions of e.g., very extreme heatwaves. In the (not directly related) <a href="https://essopenarchive.org/doi/abs/10.22541/essoar.15012112/v1">second one</a>, led by Luna Bloin-Wibe, we analyze the impact of very severe energy droughts on renewable energy systems.
